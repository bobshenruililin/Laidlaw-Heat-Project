## Attacks and disposition

1. **Could these be called cardiovascular admissions or incident disease?** No. The manuscript defines first recorded stays after relevant diagnosis, states admission cause is absent, and disclaims disease-specific incidence, first-ever disease and principal-diagnosis claims. CHD/HF totals are separate; the same person's possible contribution to both is not a unique patient count. **Passed.**
2. **Does the diagnosis interval prove a closed, depleted or clinically incident cohort?** No. Methods state that the extraction query, diagnosis setting, look-back, same-day/event rules and entry/exit are unverified. The supplied T2D/HTN interval spans the whole series; ordering of chronic-disease eligibility relative to cardiovascular diagnosis/hospitalisation is not established. The manuscript does not turn possible future ascertainment or risk-set change into a demonstrated bias mechanism. **Passed as an uncertainty statement; source verification remains open.**
3. **Does the 2023 return establish recovery in health?** No. Recovery is explicitly limited to recorded counts approaching 2019 totals. The earlier decline, the 2021 increase and the 2022 decrease remain visible, with no trend-adjusted counterfactual pandemic effect. **Passed.**
4. **Do fewer records or smaller cold coefficients establish physiological improvement?** No. The manuscript distinguishes clinical state, care contact, diagnosis recording and eligibility. Cold protection, changed susceptibility, weather exclusion and causal pandemic attribution are expressly unsupported. Overlapping fits are not independent period tests. **Passed.**
5. **Has the clinical literature been selected to support improvement?** No. Taiwan's favourable glycaemic change, Tokyo's adverse change, the Hong Kong primary-care overall null and the selected survivor adverse evidence all remain. Observation/attendance and timing restrictions are preserved. Hu's poorer measured BP control and Yau's less-conclusive stroke/mortality findings remain visible. **Passed.**
6. **Are external results imported into this population?** No. Influenza/AMI/HF associations and vaccination among infected people are contextual plausibility, not measurements or coefficients for this extract. Vaccination is not offered as the explanation for a pre-vaccination 2020 fall. **Passed.**
7. **Does linked clinical evidence eliminate selection?** No. The Yau paragraph states repeated-attendance and event-free survival selection at June 2022, and notes conditioning on infection status. It does not call retained-attender biomarkers population physiology or covariate attenuation mediation. **Passed.**
8. **Does the conceptual diagram imply identification?** The supplement caption labels relationships plausible, signs unknown and the graph incomplete for causal adjustment. No numerical cohort flow or identified pathway is claimed. **Passed at caption/semantic level; visual inspection is separate.**
