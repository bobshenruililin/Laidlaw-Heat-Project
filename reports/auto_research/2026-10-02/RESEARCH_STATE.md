## Decision-relevant diagnosis

The defensible present object is **recorded first-hospitalisation counts and the sensitivity of ecological weather count ratios to analytical choices**. The proposed COVID direction has not acquired a COVID causal estimand or evidence capable of distinguishing improved cardiovascular health from changes in recording, service use, cohort entry, or competing death. Making window dependence the preferred manuscript thesis does not resolve that problem. A useful first run therefore ends with a small, auditable evidence boundary and consequential questions, rather than another article version. [C01–C08]

## Measurement and estimands

The public receipt describes two territory-month series, January 2013–December 2023, each with 132 months: first hospitalisation after a first CHD or HF diagnosis record among people with type 2 diabetes and/or hypertension. **Admission cause is absent.** Inpatient status is inferred from delivered column names pending confirmation; ICD lists, diagnosis/admission timing, look-back/washout, deduplication, entry, exit, and recurrent-event exclusions remain incompletely documented. Monthly still-at-risk person-time, stroke, and laboratory/infection/vaccination/serology series were not delivered in this transfer. Lack of fields in this transfer is not proof they cannot be obtained. [C02, C13–C14]

Three objects must remain separate:

- **Observed burden:** annual totals of recorded first events in this extraction. These describe records, not incidence or disease-caused admissions.
- **Existing statistical parameter:** an ecological, model-conditional expected-count ratio for an exposure contrast, conditional on calendar-month indicators, `ns(time_index, 4)`, and days in month. Separate fits use 1°C for monthly temperature means and five additional official hot nights, cold days, or very hot days. Five is a scale, not consecutive duration. Without a cohort denominator, the offset is exposure time in calendar days, not person-time. [C03–C04]
- **Proposed causal target:** a pandemic-era change in cardiovascular health, utilisation, or recorded first-event occurrence. The target population, intervention/exposure regime, outcome, horizon, counterfactual and adjustment set have not been agreed. “COVID period” bundles infection, restrictions, service changes, vaccination and other shifts; it cannot substitute for that specification. [C07–C08]

The 84-month `pre_covid` model is fit on 2013–2019 contained inside the full 132 months. Script 32 filters the data before `ns`; default knots and boundary knots change with the subset. Sample, support and nuisance fit therefore change together. Correlated overlapping estimates and their interval overlap are not a test of difference. There is no reported exposure-by-period interaction or separate post-only 48-month Model 1 fit. Full-sample COVID-phase indicators are an intercept adjustment, not a third analysis window or a COVID treatment effect. [C07]

## What the accessible evidence supports

Released annual totals show CHD 23,830 in 2013, 12,396 in 2019, 10,237 in 2020, and 12,323 in 2023; HF 4,336, 2,344, 1,964, and 2,296. Most endpoint decline precedes 2020, followed by a 2020 trough and partial recovery. This weakens a story attributing the entire decade-long decline to COVID, without identifying the cause of either component. The general population aged 35+ is not the cohort risk set. [C15]

Existing lag-6 estimates move: HF cold days 1.113 (1.053–1.176) nested versus 1.073 (1.006–1.144) full; CHD cold days 1.036 (1.007–1.067) versus 0.995 (0.949–1.043); CHD hot nights 1.011 (0.991–1.032) versus 1.022 (1.002–1.042). These are traced estimates, not evidence of a statistically established window difference or biological modification. All twelve full-window BH q-values exceed 0.19; nested fits are outside that family. CHD hot-night model-based/HC1 intervals include 1 while lag-6 excludes it. [C09, C16–C17]

Primary-source neighbouring studies make utilisation and competing mortality credible rivals, without measuring this cohort: Xin used weekly 2010–2020 hospitalisations/mortality; Wai's 2022 emergency-department paper (online 2021) covered January–August 2019/2020; Wai's 2024 study used 2016–2021 emergency records and a different identification design; Hung surveyed self-reported avoidance in March–April 2020. Tam concerns STEMI care, a different clinical endpoint. These are contextual triangulation, not transferable coefficients or independent confirmation of this file's mechanism. The candidate's Xin bibliography has an error: the publication is 2023, volume 30, article 100645, with online publication in 2022. [L01–L05]

## Negative knowledge and the strength of the tests

Retain the calibration decision `FAIL_METHODS_FEASIBILITY_ONLY` as **SYNTHETIC_CALIBRATION**: the tested implementation/scenarios reached null Type I error 0.150 and minimum coverage 0.840 against stricter gates. That is evidence against admitting that implementation's daily-recovery estimates under the tested conditions, not a universal theorem about aggregation or a falsification of physiology. F1.2 re-summarised already-run simulations; its gates do not become prospective merely by being executable. [C10]

“No protected discovery” is a reporting conclusion; “no thermal effect” is unsupported. “Physiological improvement killed” is an evidential refusal on this file, not an empirical disproof of that mechanism. Closed-cohort depletion is unverified; new entrants mean no mathematical requirement of monotonically falling counts. A short post window is an anticipated precision/support problem, not proof that every post-period design is impossible. Public daily mortality cannot replace a monthly first-event outcome, although it may triangulate external consequences. [C11, C13–C14, C18]

The weather “ruling-out exhibit” lacks a specified null, a predicted pattern, a meaningful equivalence margin and an analysis shown capable of detecting that pattern. Stability or instability of a confounded, re-knotted coefficient cannot rule weather out. Its current severity is weak. Residual dependence, concentrated cold exposure and influence months deserve model criticism; residual autocorrelation alone does not prove every HAC coefficient variance should be larger than its model-based counterpart, because HAC uses score cross-products. [C08, C17–C19; L06–L07]

The old numeral auditor passes **one check** on the COVID schema: it audits `claims`, while the schema uses `numerals`. The dedicated rails check display-string presence, source existence, citation/layout contracts and frozen neighbours; they do not independently validate each CSV value or identify a causal effect. This run separately checked pivotal released numerals and BH arithmetic. Agreement verifies transcription/arithmetic, not the secure-data fits. Repeated agent judgements and green tests are not independent experiments. [C20–C21; VERIFICATION.md]

## Five consequential questions

Owners are proposed responsibilities, not new assignments or messages.

1. **What exactly generates a first event?** Roro/DAE: query/dictionary, ICD codes, dates, entry/exit, washout, deduplication and inpatient definition. Stop causal or incidence interpretation until confirmed; retain recorded-count descriptions with the uncertainty explicit.
2. **Is there a pandemic-era deviation beyond the pre-existing decline?** Research agent plus Hogan: specify a target and competing predicted time patterns before any authorized modelling; distinguish anticipation, 2020 interruption, later waves and recovery from secular drift. The annual path alone cannot identify an interruption.
3. **Can observation and disease processes be distinguished?** Roro/Bishai: assess availability and governance of risk-set person-time, service/contact records, principal diagnoses and mortality linkage; labs/infection/vaccination only if needed for a specified rival. If these cannot discriminate mechanisms, narrow to recorded burden or seek a different dataset.
4. **Why do window coefficients move?** Research agent: prepare a bounded diagnostic protocol separating changing exposure support, spline basis, influential months, serial uncertainty and genuine effect modification. Predefine meaningful contrasts and error sensitivity; do not select the specification that preserves significance.
5. **What scientific target and manuscript authority does the team actually endorse?** Bob/Hogan/Bishai: resolve the shared-document authority and choose descriptive narrowing, governed evidence acquisition, pivot or stop. Resolve this before another manuscript cycle or a Gate 3 freeze.

## Conditional recommendation and Phase 2 handoff

Retain the count path and complete exploratory sensitivity record. Deprioritise causal COVID-improvement and weather-exclusion claims until an identified target and discriminating evidence exist. Phase 2 should compare observation/risk-set change, service avoidance/competing death, biological or co-exposure change, and model/support artefacts; these can coexist. Require predictions that differ and specify what would count against each explanation (Popper/strong inference), whether the test could detect that error (severe testing), and whether the move gains new independent predictions rather than rescuing old headlines (Lakatos). A DAG should distinguish health events from recorded admissions, and diagnostics should ask what the model misses. If feasible evidence cannot separate the important rivals, descriptive narrowing or stopping is scientifically productive.
