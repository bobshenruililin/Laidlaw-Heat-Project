## 2024 is after the health window

HKO Year’s Weather 2024 (https://www.hko.gov.hk/en/wxinfo/pastwx/2024/ywx2024.htm; also GIA 13 Jan 2025): 50 hot nights, 52 very hot days, 2 extremely hot days, 11 cold days. Annual mean 24.8 °C, warmest year on record. **Do not add 2024 to the 132-month health models.** Optional Discussion context only: heat-night burden stayed high after 2023; cold days did not disappear (11 days). That is exposure natural history, not a health result.

## 2019 wording trap

The 2019 yearbook called 46 hot nights the highest on record and 1 cold day the fewest since 1884. 2021 later recorded 61 hot nights. The live paper already uses 2013 vs 2021 vs 2023, which is the right selected-year comparison. Do not freeze 2019 as the heat peak.
