# Sol journal-target recommendation

**Scope:** journal strategy for the governed CHD/HF monthly aggregate paper. This note does not freeze Gate 3, confer dissemination authority, or convert exploratory estimates into a primary claim.

## Recommendation

Submit first to **Environmental Research** once the human-owned blockers below are closed. It is the best balance of subject fit, space for identification diagnostics, and tolerance for a careful single-city environmental time-series paper whose contribution is inferential transparency rather than a protected positive finding.

An **Environmental Health Perspectives (EHP)** submission is defensible as one deliberate reach if the team accepts a substantial desk-rejection risk. **International Journal of Epidemiology (IJE)** is a credible methods-facing alternative, but the paper does not introduce a new estimator and would require severe compression. **The Lancet Planetary Health** has strong thematic fit but weak editorial fit for the present evidence. **Nature Communications** is not supported as a methods paper by a project-specific calibration failure alone. Nature, Science, and Cell are not responsible targets for these data.

## Ranked venues

| Rank | Venue | Fit | Principal risk | Practical format |
|---:|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Environmental Research** | Strong environmental epidemiology and temperature–health fit. The complete core panel, depletion display, winter-identification display, and diagnostic supplement form a coherent Research Paper. | A single territory, 132 monthly observations, no cohort risk-set denominator, unresolved confounding, and no multiplicity-protected contrast limit novelty. Reviewers may also object to inferred inpatient semantics. | Current guidance says Research Papers should preferably remain within 7,500 words excluding references, with about 5–6 figures and/or reasonably sized tables. Use three main figures and at most two main tables; move the diagnostic inventory to the supplement. |
| 2 | **Environmental Health Perspectives** | Strong environmental-health scope and a good home for explicit estimand, missingness, and covariance diagnostics. The supplement can carry extensive secondary analyses. | EHP asks for a substantial advance. The present advance is disciplined reporting, not a causal or protected thermal effect. Governed data that cannot be shared will require a precise, editor-acceptable access statement. | EHP suggests fewer than 7,000 words for Articles, excluding the abstract, references, tables, captions, acknowledgements, and supporting information. Its supplement guidance places no numerical cap on supplementary figures or tables but requires the main text to stand alone. |
| 3 | **International Journal of Epidemiology** | The estimand boundary, first-event depletion, residual dependence, and failed daily-recovery admission are epidemiologically serious. | There is no new general estimator, and the health analysis is exploratory and post-outcome specified. The single-city monthly design may be judged too narrow for IJE without a sharper general lesson about aggregated-outcome inference. | Typical Original Article limits are 3,000 main-text words, a 250-word structured abstract, and eight figures/tables combined. | 4 | **The Lancet Planetary Health** | Climate–health subject fit is high, and Hong Kong's concurrent rise in hot nights and persistence of cold days is policy-relevant context. | Editorial fit is poor at present: one city, 132 monthly units, uncertain risk set, unrecorded admission cause, all core *q*-values above 0.19, and no protected primary contrast. | 5 | **Nature Communications (methods framing)** | Aggregation-aware inference and a predeclared refusal rule could interest methods readers if supported by a broadly validated methodological contribution. | The M\|D work failed project-specific calibration and produced no admitted real daily coefficient. A negative feasibility result in one setting neither introduces a method nor establishes a general failure mode. Rebranding the health paper as methods would overstate it. | Guidance recommends no more than 5,000 words of main text excluding Abstract, Methods, References, and legends; Methods are typically below 3,000 words; up to ten display items are possible. | 6 | **Nature / Science / Cell** | None at flagship level for the present study. | These venues require a broad conceptual advance or discovery supported by unusually decisive evidence. This study has an exploratory monthly ecological panel, open human gates, unresolved outcome semantics, and no multiplicity-protected claim. CNS-quality prose does not create CNS-level evidence. | Nature typically budgets about 2,500 words and four modest display items for a six-page Article, or 4,300 words and 5–6 items for eight pages. ## Submission sequence

1. Close the human-owned blockers.
2. Assemble the three-figure main paper and diagnostic supplement in `03_sol_supplement_map.md`.
3. Submit to **Environmental Research** as a full Research Paper.
4. If the team explicitly prefers one reach attempt, substitute **EHP** at step 3; do not describe that choice as the scientifically “better” fit.
5. Use **IJE** only if the team is willing to recast the paper around general lessons for monthly aggregate epidemiology and cut the main text to its format.
6. Do not submit to Nature Communications, Nature, Science, or Cell on the present evidence.

The repository Markdown word counts on 15 August 2026 were 5,302 for the live collaborative draft and 4,395 for the methods-heavy repository draft. Those counts include Markdown tables, references, and captions, so they are not journal word counts. They show that IJE and Lancet-family formats would require a deliberate structural cut.

## Cover letter: claims that are supportable

The cover letter can state that the study:

- analyses 132 territory-months from January 2013 to December 2023 using governed monthly aggregates;
- examines first recorded hospitalisation after first CHD or HF diagnosis among people diagnosed with type 2 diabetes and/or hypertension;
- reports a complete twelve-contrast, separate-exposure negative-binomial core panel rather than selecting the largest estimates;
- reports model-based, HC1, Newey–West lag-3, and Newey–West lag-6 uncertainty, with the direction of interval changes disclosed;
- shows that the CHD hot-night interval depends on covariance construction and is compatible with 1 before 2020;
- shows that the HF cold-day association is concordant across the four standard-error methods but remains unprotected by multiplicity;
- locates the cold-day contrast in between-winter variation and visualises the decline in first-event counts against the general population aged 35 years or older;
- applies a prospective admission rule to a synthetic daily-recovery calibration and reports the refusal to produce a real daily coefficient after calibration failure; and
- contributes an auditable example of what monthly aggregate climate–health data can and cannot identify.

A defensible cover-letter sentence is:

> We report a complete exploratory panel of ecological monthly count-ratio estimates, together with the depletion, seasonal-identification, residual-dependence, multiplicity, and calibration diagnostics needed to interpret them without promoting a single contrast to a protected finding.

## Cover letter: claims that are not supportable

Do not claim:

- a causal effect of heat or cold;
- cohort incidence or an incidence-rate ratio;
- principal-diagnosis CHD, HF, or acute myocardial infarction admissions;
- a daily trigger, distributed-lag response, or recovered daily coefficient;
- that CHD responds to heat whereas HF responds to cold as a protected differential finding;
- multiplicity protection, confirmation, replication, or a pre-specified primary health contrast;
- that Newey–West intervals are necessarily wider or automatically preferable;
- that the M\|D calibration proves the Basagaña–Ballester method fails generally;
- stroke, daily mortality, excess-death, or health-economic findings;
- complete control of pollution, influenza, humidity, depletion, or pandemic-era care-seeking;
- stable cohort person-time;
- a Nature-level discovery, methodological breakthrough, or “first” without a separate documented priority audit; or
- authority to disseminate the governed aggregates before written approval.

## Human-owned submission blockers

| Blocker | Required human decision or text | Why submission must wait |
|---|---|---|
| Written dissemination authority | Written approval from the outcome and supervising investigators for external journal submission and the proposed disclosure-minimised displays. | Aggregation reduces disclosure risk; it does not grant publication authority. |
| PI IRB decision | The supervising investigator records whether the existing protocol covers this analysis or requires an amendment. | The manuscript cannot resolve protocol scope by prose. |
| ICD and inpatient semantics | The outcome co-investigator confirms the field-level ICD construction, executable event-timing rule, and whether `*_inpatient` supports an inpatient-only statement. | Admission cause is absent, and care-setting semantics are currently inferred from labels. |
| Authorship and order | The team confirms author list, order, corresponding author, contributions, and any co-first designation. | The repository placeholders are not submission-ready authorship. |
| Cohort risk set | The outcome and supervising investigators decide whether a monthly still-at-risk denominator can be supplied. If not, they explicitly lock the count-ratio estimand. | General-population denominators do not convert the result to cohort incidence. |
| Hogan weather lock | The weather co-investigator confirms the source wording and any reference-period, tie, gap, missingness, or month-assignment rules retained for submission. | Provisional HM/CM indicators remain supplementary and cannot become a headline by editorial phrasing. |
| Roro health-data paragraph | The outcome co-investigator replaces or confirms the provisional health-data Methods with the governed construction that can be disclosed. | The present paragraph deliberately marks unresolved semantics. |
| Gate 3 | The full team chooses the final headline and claim tier after reviewing the complete panel and diagnostics. | This note recommends a venue; it does not freeze Gate 3. |
