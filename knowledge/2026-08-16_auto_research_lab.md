# 16 August 2026 — Auto-research lab (Jin pattern, epidemiology stop rule)

**Mode:** Ship. **Not a Gate 3 freeze. Not a Crouzeix proof search.**

Shanmu Jin’s Crouzeix run (GPT-5.6-Sol, ~16 hours, public prompt + Lean axiom audit) is the method analogue. The transferable engine is a virtual research institute: branching approach families, an adversary that may kill only with a table contradiction or a forbidden-claim hit, open provenance, and a stop rule that forbids “best effort” science.

The non-transferable move is “assume a complete affirmative proof exists.” All twelve core *q* > 0.19. The honest target remains an identification article.
