## Decision

**Primary: Scientific Reports. Backup: PLOS ONE.** Submit neither until the outcome co-investigator verifies the extraction definition and eligibility rules, the humans confirm ethics and dissemination facts, and a truthful data-access statement is available. No new physiological finding, causal pandemic effect or change in thermal susceptibility should be offered as the reason for submission.

The article's defensible contribution is a recorded-burden description with transparent model criticism: a decline already present before 2020, a further 2020 dip, and return towards 2019 recorded totals by 2023. The complete thermal panel is exploratory, multiplicity-adjusted results are inconclusive, and coefficients depend on calendar control and analysis window. This is a narrower contribution than resolving whether cardiovascular health improved. Its clinical outcome construction and novelty must be defended directly rather than compensated for by more elaborate figures.

## Why these venues

**Scientific Reports** covers natural and clinical sciences and assesses technical soundness and scientific validity rather than perceived importance. It explicitly accommodates negative results and justified replication. That editorial model matches a well-documented descriptive time series and a complete, inconvenient sensitivity panel, provided their measurement and inference are credible. It is a Nature Portfolio journal; its relationship to the publisher does not make this evidence equivalent to a selective Nature research-journal contribution. [Official scope](https://www.nature.com/srep/about); [referee criteria](https://www.nature.com/srep/guide-to-referees); [editorial process](https://www.nature.com/srep/about/editorial-process).

**PLOS ONE** considers original medical research and null results, requires technical adequacy and supported conclusions, and requires a sound rationale for work similar to prior publications. This makes it a reasonable backup for the same honest contribution. It is not a route around incomplete methods or source ambiguity. The close Hong Kong diabetes and hypertension studies must be acknowledged, and the distinct first-postdiagnosis hospitalisation construct must have a scientific rationale. [Official publication criteria](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/s/criteria-for-publication).

No submission fee, waiver eligibility, timing or acceptance probability is assumed. Venue-specific formatting and final declarations should follow the selected journal's current instructions when human submission decisions are made.

## Leading-family benchmark

**Nature Cardiovascular Research is topically appropriate but not recommended for this frozen evidence.** Its scope includes cardiovascular clinical observations and public health and emphasises original, important advances. Scope fit is satisfied; the present extract does not discriminate health improvement from care and ascertainment, identify a causal pandemic contrast, establish clinical incidence, or validate a change in weather association. The close clinical studies already examine richer cardiovascular, mortality and care-use trajectories. No editorial polish can supply the missing advance. This is an assessment of this evidence, not a prediction about the journal's editors. [Official aims and scope](https://www.nature.com/natcardiovascres/aims).

## Submission conditions shared by both recommended venues

1. Verify the implemented diagnosis codes, first-event and same-day rules, look-back, inpatient setting and timing of diabetes/hypertension eligibility. The receipt is a supplied description, not the extraction query. If verification contradicts the frozen endpoint, stop promotion and resolve the freeze issue separately; do not silently change the analysis.
2. State the event as a first recorded stay after diagnosis. Do not call it a cause-coded cardiovascular admission, incident disease, first-ever disease or an incidence rate. Explain unknown cohort entry, exit and deaths without asserting a particular artefact occurred.
3. Obtain human confirmation of ethics approval or a documented exemption, data-use/publication authority, authorship, funding and competing interests. AI-assisted drafting and analysis support require accurate disclosure under the chosen journal's policy; AI systems are not authors.
4. Provide an institutional/custodian access route and actual restrictions for the governed monthly inputs. Released summaries reproduce the displays but do not enable primary model refitting. Do not promise unrestricted underlying data or invent a request contact. PLOS permits justified legal/ethical and third-party restrictions but requires an explanation and access information. [PLOS data policy](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/s/data-availability); [Scientific Reports policies](https://www.nature.com/srep/journal-policies/editorial-policies).
5. Keep prospective interaction/calibration scripts separate from completed analyses. Source audits, figure reconstruction and internal hostile review are not clinical validation or primary-data replication.
