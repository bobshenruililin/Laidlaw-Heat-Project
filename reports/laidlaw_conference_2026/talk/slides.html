Equation (1) · Model 1

One weather variable. Four moving parts.

Negative binomial. Calendar month plus a 4-df time spline. Days-in-month offset.

Offset: days in the month. Not a population. Not still-at-risk person-time.

Season: January is the reference month. This is not the weather.

Slow drift across eleven years. Four degrees of freedom. Not a heatwave length.

The number we report is \(e^{\beta}\): a monthly count ratio. One \(X_t\) per fit.

one weather variable per fit.

\log \mathrm{E}(Y_t) = \log(d_t) + \alpha + \beta X_t + \sum_{m=2}^{12} \gamma_m I(\mathrm{month}_t = m) + s(t; 4~\mathrm{df})
