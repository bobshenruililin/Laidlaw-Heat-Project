# Hogan card — a mode is not a hot night

**Date:** 14 August 2026.  
**Audience:** Hogan (weather lock). Exposure only. No coefficients.

A field laboratory on the same 0.2° ERA5-Land Pearl River Delta cache (2013–2023, 90 cells) decomposes *de-seasoned* daily Tmin. The first EOF is a **basin-wide pulse** (92% of variance; all loadings positive). It is larger inland (latitude r = +0.78) and **anti-correlated** with ERA5 nights `Tmin ≥ 28°C` (r = −0.93). Eleven yearly means give a median Theil–Sen slope of +0.29°C/decade; only 23 of 90 cells have a 90% bootstrap interval that excludes zero, and the signs split. That is not a climate-change sentence for the live Methods.
