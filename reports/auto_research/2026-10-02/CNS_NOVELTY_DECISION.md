# Contribution decision after the targeted novelty audit

**Date:** 2 October 2026. **Disposition:** expanded study design is ready to specify; expanded empirical contribution is not established. No new health transfer or approved output was supplied.

## Decision

Proceed with a governed feasibility and measurement-design packet while retaining the current manuscript as exploratory evidence. The leading-family target remains conditional. The existing count series cannot determine whether physiology improved, admissions were deferred, or eligibility and recording changed.

The broad diabetes/hypertension pandemic-and-care question is already studied locally. The newest continuity paper also links care changes to subsequent CHD/HF and uses clinical covariates. A plan whose novelty is simply “add labs, mortality and longer follow-up” is therefore insufficient. [Targeted audit](../../../literature/covid_novelty_audit_2026-10-02.md), [Youn 2025](https://doi.org/10.1002/hsr2.71291), [Hu 2025](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41440-025-02230-y), [Yau 2026](https://doi.org/10.1111/dom.70984).

## Preferred candidate contribution

Determine whether the apparent improvement survives a change from diagnosis-associated hospital stays to validated cardiovascular events with matched eligible person-time. Then ask whether observed clinical, care-contact and recording trajectories make different predictions across disruption and recovery. A coherent finding must survive event-definition criticism and reproduce a prespecified contrast in another governed setting.

This is a candidate scientific question. It is neither a claim of being first nor a promise that the observation processes can be causally decomposed. Full texts and decisive supplements of the closest studies must be reconciled before promoting a novelty statement.

## Executable decisions

1. **Current records only:** complete descriptive and model-criticism work; use recorded-burden language. Do not claim physiological benefit, causal pandemic effect or a ruled-out weather contribution.
2. **Validated events plus eligible person-time:** permit an expanded incidence study after schema/query verification and a prospectively frozen analysis plan for new associations. Do not call this sufficient evidence of a mechanism.
3. **Linked clinical and care measurements:** add mechanism discrimination only when dates, units, testing/attendance selection, treatment and missingness can be evaluated. Retained-attender improvement alone fails this criterion.
4. **External cohort:** harmonise the target and phenotype before inspecting validation associations. A holdout hospital cluster is internal replication; official mortality comparisons are triangulation.
5. **Insufficient discrimination or duplication:** narrow the manuscript and journal target. Do not rescue the original claim by selecting a cut date, exposure, spline, subgroup or attractive interval.

## Release conditions

For an expanded manuscript, require documented extraction semantics; verified denominators and competing deaths; all declared contrasts; calibration and model criticism; transparent testing-selection limits; complete citation checks; and genuinely independent validation or a clearly narrowed generalisability claim. Human submission and dissemination decisions stay separate from this scientific packet.
