# Laidlaw website blog canon — 26 July 2026

**Decision:** Bob wants Laidlaw Scholars website posts in a quirky, imaginative, human first-person voice modelled on **MIT Admissions Blogs**. The blog may be warm and intellectually playful, but it must remain specific, scientifically honest, and recognisably Bob—not corporate copy and not manufactured whimsy.

## Register split

- **Laidlaw website blog:** process, anticipation, revision, collaboration, waiting, identity, and the odd poetry of methods. Short paragraphs; general-reader explanations; collaborators credited naturally.
- **CNS / Stage 3 / manuscript writing:** spare publication register, formal evidence, explicit estimands, and the standards in `analysis_plan/writing_standards_hogan.md` plus the CNS-writing skill.

Scientific boundaries do not change with register. Do not invent biography, private dialogue, HA fields, coefficients, or findings. Monthly stroke aggregates are not daily mortality or a daily DLNM. Published cold/heat evidence and exposure-only weather summaries must be labelled as context, not project health results.

## Canonical first posts

1. [`reports/blog/week1_recalibration.md`](../reports/blog/week1_recalibration.md) — *My Research Question Changed Its Mind*: the 17 July recalibration from AMI to an honest monthly, multi-method project.
2. [`reports/blog/week2_definitions_and_waiting.md`](../reports/blog/week2_definitions_and_waiting.md) — *How Many Ways Can a Month Be Hot?*: definitions as recipes, Hogan/Roro teamwork, and waiting before outcome inference.
3. [`reports/blog/week3_methods_while_waiting.md`](../reports/blog/week3_methods_while_waiting.md) — *The Results Section Was Blank*: writing Methods into a shared manuscript while Results stayed empty.
4. [`reports/blog/week4_then_there_was_something_to_run.md`](../reports/blog/week4_then_there_was_something_to_run.md) — *Then There Was Something To Run*: CHD/HF first-event counts arrive; stroke still absent; the prepared panel can be run. No coefficients.

The withdrawn inventory draft `week_of_data_the_file_that_arrived.md` is not a model. Do not restore it.

Directory guidance: [`reports/blog/README.md`](../reports/blog/README.md) and [`reports/blog/STYLE_NOTES.md`](../reports/blog/STYLE_NOTES.md).

Involve Bob earlier in the creative process: ask what moment, image, discomfort, or change in anticipation felt most true before drafting. Repository notes can protect factual accuracy, but they should not substitute for Bob's own selection of the story. Bob edits every draft before posting to the Laidlaw site.
