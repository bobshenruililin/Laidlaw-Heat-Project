## Scientific reading

The pre-2020 84 months are contained in the 132-month full window. The two
estimates are not independent. Interval overlap is not a difference test, and
no separate 2020–2023 Model 1 or exposure-by-period interaction is reported.

First-event counts declined substantially before 2020 and dipped further in
2020. The later period was hotter, while official cold-day frequency was
similar. The paper separates three alternatives: utilisation, depletion of the
first-event risk set, and weather entanglement. The thermal variables do not
supply a complete explanation and are not evidence that weather was
irrelevant.

Primary-source review confirmed the Xin/Wai/Hung/Tam utilisation and delay
spine and Cowling’s influenza-suppression context. Antibody production,
infection-acquired immunity, vaccination, or hybrid immunity was killed as an
explanation of the 2020 trough: the panel contains none of those measures, and
the closest studies concern cardiovascular outcomes after documented
infection.
