# Draft cover letter — *International Journal of Biometeorology*

**Bob must review and send.**

Dear Editor,

Please consider our manuscript, “Analysis-window sensitivity of first coronary
and heart-failure hospitalisation counts across pandemic-era disruption in
Hong Kong, 2013–2023.”

The study examines 132 monthly counts of first hospitalisation after first
coronary heart disease or heart-failure diagnosis among people with type 2
diabetes and/or hypertension. We report separate negative-binomial models for
six thermal encodings, a four-construction uncertainty ladder, and sensitivity
to a nested window ending in December 2019.

The contribution is an aggregation-aware account of what these monthly
first-event series can and cannot establish. First-event counts declined before
and during pandemic-era disruption. Cold-day point estimates were larger in
the nested pre-2020 window than in the full window, but the estimates are not
independent, their intervals overlap, and no exposure-by-period interaction was
fitted. Later years were hotter while official cold-day frequency was similar.
The observed thermal variables do not supply a complete explanation for the
count trajectory.

The manuscript reports the complete twelve-fit panel, including null and
discordant results. All full-window multiplicity-adjusted *q*-values exceed
0.19. It also shows residual serial dependence, influence-month checks, and the
limits imposed by an unavailable still-at-risk denominator and absent
admission cause. We believe this transparent treatment of temporal
aggregation, weather encoding, and pandemic-era disruption will interest the
journal’s readers.

The manuscript has not been submitted by an agent. Before submission, the
authors must confirm authorship, the ethics reference, dissemination authority,
conflict statements, and exclusive-submission status.
