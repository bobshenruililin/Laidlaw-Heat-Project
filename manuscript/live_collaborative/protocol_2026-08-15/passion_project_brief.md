# Passion-project brief — separate from the live manuscript

**Rule.** Wonder here. Do not contaminate Results, Abstract, or the one-sentence thesis.

The present HA file supports an **epidemiological count-ratio panel**. It does not support a cost-effectiveness analysis. There is no decision problem with a priced intervention, no LOS or unit cost, no QALY, and no policy counterfactual in the aggregates. Hospital counts are utilisation, but utilisation without valuation is still epidemiology.

## Track D — scientific passions worth a PhD-scale sequel

- Mechanism: nighttime intensity, sleep, blood pressure — needs individual or at least daily data.
- Adaptation: time-varying heat RR in a depleting first-event cohort is confounded by construction.
- Equity: WorldPop-weighted nights are an exposure map, not a Marmot finding, until health is linked under governance.
- Methods: when monthly official counts identify intensity rather than occurrence, and how to report that as the result rather than as a footnote.
