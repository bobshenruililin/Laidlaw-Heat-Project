# STORM perspectives

Each perspective is a *role the text must survive*, not a co-author.

## 1. Identification epidemiologist (this panel)

**Job:** Keep the estimand. Month counts of official warning days among T2D/HTN first-hospitalisation CHD/HF. Admission cause absent. Models 2–3 unfitted. BH q > 0.19. CHD lag-1 residual ACF 0.508.

**Will kill the draft if:** physiology is written as identified; 5-day consecutive duration is inferred from `I(count/5)`; Wang/Ho/Law 2026 mortality is collapsed into this panel; Gate 3 jargon appears in the scientific body.

## 2. Cardiovascular / thermal physiologist

**Job:** Offer *candidate mechanisms* that could make nights matter for CHD more than very hot days, and cold days matter for HF, without claiming the 132 months measured them.

**Sources they may use:** Chevance et al. 2024 (sleep); Ioannou et al. 2024 (chamber nights, core temperature and TST); O’Connor et al. 2025 (older adults, bedroom temperature and HRV); Ashe et al. 2025 (systematic review: only three EHE–sleep–CVD studies); Guo et al. 2024 (Hong Kong: official hot-night flag often null after mean T; hourly night excess associated); Ikäheimo 2018 and Li et al. 2026 (cold, vasoconstriction, afterload, HF); Goggins & Chan 2017 (Hong Kong daily HF admissions, different estimand).

**Will kill the draft if:** a non-dipping BP theorem is asserted from Ashe’s three studies; chamber n=7 is treated as the Hong Kong T2D/HTN population; indoor bedroom temperature is claimed from outdoor warning counts.

## 3. Heat–health action-plan planner (WHO 2026)

**Job:** Map residuals onto the eight core elements of *Heat–health action plans: guidance*, 2nd ed. (WHO Regional Office for Europe, 2026): governance; heat–health warning system; populations at increased risk; communication; health-system resilience; reducing heat exposure; heat–health surveillance; monitoring, evaluation and learning.

**Will kill the draft if:** the paper claims Hong Kong lacks an HHAP (it has a warning/communication/shelter bundle); if the paper claims this panel should set a consecutive-day trigger; if cold is omitted (WHO heat guidance does not replace a cold-weather plan).

## 4. Hong Kong operations reader (HKO / DH / HAD)

**Job:** Name the actual instruments: Very Hot Weather Warning; Hot Weather Special Advisory; Prolonged Heat Special Alert (issued when VHWW is in force *and* a few days of very hot days *or* hot nights are expected or occurring); Extremely Hot Weather Special Alert; HKHI; Labour Heat Stress at Work Warning; DH/CHP heat-stroke advice (elderly; chronic illness including heart disease and high blood pressure); HAD temporary heat shelters; Cold Weather Warning (separate).

**Will kill the draft if:** Prolonged Heat is described as a five-consecutive-night rule; VHWW is described as a fixed consecutive-day heatwave; this panel is said to have evaluated any of these products.
