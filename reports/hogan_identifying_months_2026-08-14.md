# Hogan card — what survives month indicators

**Date:** 14 August 2026.  
**Audience:** Hogan (weather lock) and, later, a methods limitation. Exposure only.

The live paper already notes that official cold days are concentrated in December–February. The same month-factor arithmetic applies to all six encodings:

- Monthly **means** are almost entirely seasonal. After calendar-month indicators, **4–5%** of the 132-month sum of squares remains.
- **Hot nights** and **very hot days** vary in May–October only. Identifying shares **29%** and **33%**.
- **Cold days** vary in December–March. Identifying share **47%** — larger because the series is already winter-shaped, not because the contrast is strong.

This is why a twelve-contrast panel can be well specified and still report all core *q* > 0.19. Multiplicity is not the only reason.

Keep Headquarters. Do not swap the encoding to gain identifying months.
