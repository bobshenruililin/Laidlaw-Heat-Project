# Scientific research workflow and reviewer risks

## Adopt individual practices

The [EvoSkills](https://github.com/EvoScientist/EvoSkills) [paper-review](https://github.com/EvoScientist/EvoSkills/blob/main/skills/paper-review/SKILL.md) and [figure-specification](https://github.com/EvoScientist/EvoSkills/blob/main/skills/paper-figures/SKILL.md) workflows inform explicit claim-to-test links, figure purposes, source checks and rejection-first review. [Autoresearch](https://github.com/karpathy/autoresearch) informs bounded experiments with fixed evaluations. No framework is installed. Repository architecture is retained.

Scientific evaluation is error detection, interval calibration, meaningful precision and discrimination among rival predictions. Neither p-values nor an agent's manuscript score are optimisation targets. A hypothesis protected by changing specifications after seeing results does not gain evidence.

## Bounded research cycle

1. Name one unresolved scientific decision and its claim, population, estimand, source and adverse prediction. State what would leave it inconclusive.
2. Verify measurement, exposure support and governance. Define the complete contrast family, calendar alternatives, missingness/selection strategy, calibration scenarios and validation reservation before new associations are inspected.
3. The analyst implements inside the approved environment, using versioned explicit inputs and outputs. Preserve failures and reasons. Never silently replace failed fits or discard unfavourable contrasts.
4. A separate reviewer attacks measurement, identification, selection, support, dependence, model predictions, precision, discordant results, source accuracy and novelty. Simulation assesses whether the test could detect its intended error; simulated outputs remain SYNTHETIC.
5. Resolve material defects and rerun affected checks. Retain unresolved empirical limitations visibly. A routine repair cycle ends when its declared checks pass; further searching requires a new scientific question and a record of why it matters.
6. Update the claim ledger and existing briefing at a substantive decision. Promote only verified claims. Freeze primary headlines through the project's human Gate 3 process; a script or review score cannot perform that freeze.

The manuscript ledger binds current quantitative statements to approved aggregates. The protocol's rival predictions specify prospective tests. Before linked-data execution, extend the ledger with prespecified identifiers, achieved precision, missingness/selection diagnostics, calibration, adverse observations and human disposition. The current ledger is not a new primary-data replication.

## Negative knowledge to retain

Nested pre84/full132 contrasts do not estimate a pre/post effect. A days-in-month offset does not create person-time. The event is not validated principal-diagnosis CHD/HF incidence. No laboratory transfer, direct period interaction or independent validation has arrived. Calendar sensitivity and multiplicity weaken simple thermal headlines. Better measurements alone are not new relative to the closest studies. Preserve these decisions; do not repeatedly reopen them through prose or specification search.

Legacy daily-effect recovery, unrelated pathways, old manuscript wordsmithing and synthetic health-economics exercises are not candidate findings for this manuscript. Keep their historical records in place without importing them into the current claim family.

## Reviewer risks and acceptance

**Measurement, highest priority:** event cause, first-observed eligibility, diagnosis timing, look-back and source query remain unverified. The current paper labels them; incidence claims require reconciliation and denominator/death linkage.

**Novelty, highest priority:** diabetes, hypertension and continuity-of-care studies already combine clinical and care evidence. Full texts and supplements not yet checked remain an open novelty gate. The candidate observation-process comparison is not established globally unique.

**Identification:** calendar changes combine policy, care, infection, vaccination and secular trends. Direct weather interactions describe association; coefficient changes after adjustment do not establish mediation. A graph is a statement of assumptions, not proof that they hold.

**Selection and missingness:** repeated values among surviving attenders can improve when less healthy patients disappear. Evaluate attendance, testing, treatment and death together; report bounds or sensitivity where selection cannot be repaired.

**Inference and model criticism:** existing fits are calendar-sensitive and nominal HAC uncertainty is not certified. The new runner is statically reviewed but unexecuted in this environment. Require finite-sample calibration, influential-month checks, adequate support and consequential sensitivity reporting before promoting new estimates.

**Validation:** no external cohort is confirmed. Hospital holdout is internal validation; public mortality is triangulation. Harmonised outcomes and transportability must be shown, with validation results reserved until primary freeze.

**Current disposition:** a reviewable exploratory manuscript, supplement, four figures and ten-slide briefing are complete. A CNS-family submission is not yet justified. Acceptance still requires verified extraction definitions, any new analyses and calibration, visible null/discordant results, resolved material reviews and human confirmation of ethics, dissemination, authorship and submission facts. [Nature Cardiovascular Research scope](https://www.nature.com/natcardiovascres/aims) is the initial fit benchmark; select the venue from completed evidence.
