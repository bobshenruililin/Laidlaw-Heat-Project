Laidlaw Heat Project

Public companions

Exposure descriptives only. No project health coefficients on these pages. These are the maps and ribbons a Laidlaw website post or LSN summary can link after Bob’s privacy pass.

Instrument

The threshold does not travel

Move the night. At 28°C, HKO records 449, ERA5 at the same coordinate records 17, apparent temperature records 1,453. Jaccard 0.031. Step through the 14 nights both thermometers called hot, and the 71 station nights already ≥29°C that ERA5 still missed. Not a hospital map.

Field laboratory

A field has modes

Eight methods on one ERA5 Tmin field. EOF1 is a basin-wide pulse (92% of de-seasoned variance). The 28°C night is still a marine climatology. Not a hospital map.

Geospatial model

The river has a temperature

A 0.2° ERA5 mosaic of the Pearl River Delta. Marine-south cells collect ~353 nights ≥28°C; Guangzhou’s box collects ~12. Spatial lag ρ = 0.45. Not a hospital map.

Hong Kong lattice

Hong Kong is not a point

A 0.1° ERA5-Land lattice over the territory. Waglan’s 252 hot nights against Headquarters’ 17. Elevation and a north–south trend, not a district health map.

Peer cities

Hong Kong is not Singapore

Sixteen cities on one public temperature grid — and the HKO 28°C night that does not survive reanalysis.
