## How the maps become research

The live paper already says Headquarters is applied territory-wide. That was a hedge. It is now a measured claim.

1. **Guo 2024** showed the official 28°C *flag* is the wrong metric next to hourly excess (null hospitalization association for HNday28°C; intensity is not).
2. **Mistry 2022** showed ERA5-Land *means* are usually good enough for mortality curves, with weaker heat tails in the tropics.
3. **This internship** showed the official flag is also the wrong *instrument and place*: daily Jaccard 0.031; monthly slope 0.045; marine geography; Waglan ≠ Headquarters even on the same grid.

The CHD/HF panel stays exploratory (all core q > 0.19). The maps do not rescue a headline. They explain why Headquarters was the right encoding *and* why a null-ish hot-night interval is not a surprise: only 29% of HKO hot-night variation survives month indicators, and ERA5 would have given us nine months with any 28°C night.

That is multi-method in Bishai’s sense: one health panel, one identification panel, one refusal to mix them.
