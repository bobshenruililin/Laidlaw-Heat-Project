# Pre- vs post-COVID “health improvement” — mechanism memo (quick search)

**Date:** 10 September 2026. **Audience:** Bob / later Sol. **Not** a finding from our panel.

Hogan asked for a sense of what evidence and mechanisms other people found that might explain a peculiar pre- vs post-COVID trend. It does not have to be CHD/HF.

## What our file can show

Window-dependent **count ratios** and a **halving of first-event counts**. It cannot show physiological recovery. It has no labs, no person-time, no admission cause.

## Local literature (Hong Kong)

**Utilisation down, deaths up — opposite of physiological improvement.**

- Xin H, et al. Hospitalizations and mortality during the first year of the COVID-19 pandemic in Hong Kong. *Lancet Reg Health West Pac.* 2022. DOI: [10.1016/j.lanwpc.2022.100645](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.lanwpc.2022.100645). HKUMed summary: ~359,790 fewer public hospitalisations in 2020 (largely respiratory and cardiovascular) and additional deaths, particularly cardiovascular; deaths outside public hospitals rose. [Press note](https://sph.hku.hk/en/News-And-Events/Press-Releases/2022/HKUMed-researchers-found--a-reduction-in-hospitalisations).
- Hung KK, et al. Changes in emergency department visits … associated with the COVID-19 pandemic. *Ann Emerg Med.* 2022. DOI: [10.1016/j.annemergmed.2021.09.424](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.annemergmed.2021.09.424). CHD visits down through the study window.
- Wong ELY, et al. The effect of the COVID-19 pandemic on non–COVID-19 deaths. *J Med Internet Res.* 2023. DOI: [10.2196/41792](https://doi.org/10.2196/41792). ED visits down; 28-day non-COVID mortality up.
- Hung KK, et al. Health service utilization in Hong Kong during the COVID-19 pandemic. *Int J Health Policy Manag.* Survey: reported reductions in ED and admission use.

**Do not import Xin’s 359,790 or excess deaths into Table 2.** Different estimand, different population, not our first-event T2D/HTN construction.

## Mechanisms that can look like “improvement” in counts

1. **Hospital avoidance / delayed presentation** (Xin; Hung; Wong). Counts fall; community deaths can rise.
2. **First-event depletion** in a 2013–2023 diagnosis window (already live Figure 1). The at-risk set shrinks; this is not incidence.
3. **Influenza and other respiratory co-circulation** collapsed in 2020 in many settings; our flu series is 121/132 months and is a sensitivity, not Model 1.
4. **Pollution decline** 2013–2023 (NO2 53.7→32.1; PM2.5 30.8→14.6 µg m−3) is already in the parked Introduction. Slow, not a 2020-only physiology switch.
5. **Coding and service mix** (Playbook 99 case 4): verify before biology.

## Mechanisms that point the other way

- SARS-CoV-2 cardiac injury and long COVID literature is a **harm** story, not a recovery story.
- Deferred elective and emergency cardiac care (STEMI delay notes in local ED work).

## Honest sentence for the COVID-period Discussion

Other people’s Hong Kong files show fewer hospitalisations and more cardiovascular deaths in 2020. That is a care-seeking and system-shock pattern. It is not evidence that cold days repaired hearts in our T2D/HTN first-event panel.
