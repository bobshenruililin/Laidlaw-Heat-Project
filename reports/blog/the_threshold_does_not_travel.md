# The Threshold Does Not Travel

A 28°C night in Hong Kong is an official object. The Observatory counts it at Headquarters. Our paper uses that count.

I asked the same step of a public reanalysis at the same coordinate. Four thousand and seventeen days later, the station had 449 such nights and the grid had 17. They agreed on 14. Apparent temperature, on the same grid, invented 1,453.

That is not a rounding error. Jaccard overlap is 0.83 if you ask about 24°C nights, and 0.031 if you ask about 28°C nights. To make the grid *count* 449, you have to lower the night to 26.4°C. Those are not the same 449 nights.

Move the threshold on a map of the Pearl River Delta and the remaining 28°C nights sit on the water. Guangzhou’s cell keeps 23. Waglan’s keeps 423. The live paper should stay on Headquarters. Exporting the flag is already a spatial claim.

I am not posting a hospitalisation coefficient. I am posting a warning about objects.
