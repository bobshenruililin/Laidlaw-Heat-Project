# Hogan card — means travel; the flag does not

**Date:** 14 August 2026.  
**Audience:** Hogan (weather lock) and, later, a methods limitation. Exposure only.

The live paper already says Headquarters is applied territory-wide. At the **monthly grain of the models** (132 months):

- Mean Tmin, station vs ERA5 at the same coordinate: r = **0.986**, slope **1.03**.
- Official hot-night *counts*: slope **0.045** (SE 0.007). Headquarters has 28°C nights in 57 months; ERA5 in 9.

Daily Jaccard 0.031 was not an aggregation artefact. Apparent temperature overshoots (mean 11.0 vs 3.4 nights/month). Cold days inflate on ERA5 (slope 1.54), consistent with the −1.47°C night bias.

With calendar-month indicators, only **29%** of HKO hot-night variation is between-year within month, and only May–October vary at all.

Keep Headquarters. Use ERA5 for same-source geography, not as a substitute flag.
