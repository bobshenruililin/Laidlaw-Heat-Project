The river has a temperature.

Hong Kong sits at the mouth of a delta. The peer-city atlas treated Shenzhen and Guangzhou as points. This page paints the water between them: a 0.2° ERA5-Land mosaic, a spatial lag model, and a month slider that shows where a 28°C night actually lives — mostly on the sea.
