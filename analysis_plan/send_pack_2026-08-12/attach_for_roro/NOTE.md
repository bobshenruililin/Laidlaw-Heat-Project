# HKO Headquarters monthly temperature panel, 2013–2023

Public climate only. No Hospital Authority columns.

**File:** `temperature_monthly_panel_2013_2023.csv`  
**Grain:** calendar month, January 2013–December 2023 (132 months)  
**Station:** Hong Kong Observatory Headquarters

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| month_id | YYYY-MM |
| mean_temp, mean_tmin, mean_tmax | Monthly means of daily mean / minimum / maximum temperature (°C) |
| hot_nights | Days with Tmin ≥ 28°C |
| very_hot_days | Days with Tmax ≥ 33°C |
| extremely_hot_days | Days with Tmax ≥ 35°C |
| cold_days | Days with Tmin ≤ 12°C |
| days_in_2d3n_window | Days belonging to a two-very-hot-days / three-hot-nights window |
| station | HKO |

Thresholds follow HKO climatological conventions. Monthly counts are the project’s roll-up of those daily flags.
