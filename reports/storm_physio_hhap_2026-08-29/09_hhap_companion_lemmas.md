## WHO 2026 required OUTPUTS (executive summary, ISBN 9789289062930)

Quoted from WHO Regional Office for Europe HHAP guidance 2nd ed. executive summary (“Building blocks of an HHAP”):

1. Governance: “an agreed governance structure with clear roles and responsibilities, including an identified lead body and a coordination mechanism or group to initiate, develop and implement the HHAP”
2. Heat–health warning system: “an operational heat–health warning system, linking each alert level to predefined measures with assigned responsibilities and implementation timelines”
3. Populations at increased risk: “an assessment and mapping of relevant populations at increased risk of heat related harm … together with a specific outreach strategy”
4. Communication: “a documented heat–health communication strategy that outlines the communications objectives, activities, allocated resources and agreed responsibilities”
5. Health system resilience: “a strategy and implementation framework for health system resilience … and operational plans and business continuity arrangements”
6. Reducing heat exposure: “a roadmap for heat exposure reduction that maps relevant and feasible interventions … household, building and urban”
7. Heat–health surveillance: “an operational heat–health surveillance system with defined indicators, data sources, reporting timelines and data-sharing arrangements, linked to the heat–health warning system”
8. Monitoring, evaluation and learning: “a functional monitoring, evaluation and learning framework that supports systematic review, accountability and continuous improvement”

URL: `https://www.who.int/europe/publications/i/item/9789289062930`  
Exec summary PDF: `https://www.who.int/docs/librariesprovider2/default-document-library/who-hhap-guidance-exec-summary-eng-2026.pdf`

## 4. Element 8 evaluation design — future work only

Required for a real MEL evaluation (not this paper’s result):

1. **Daily** cause-recorded admissions (or equivalent dated morbidity) — not monthly first-event aggregates without admission cause.
2. A **predeclared** confirmatory contrast (exposure definition, lag, population, multiplicity plan) before looking at estimates.
3. **Warning-day exposure** (VHWW / Prolonged Heat / Extremely Hot / advisory days as treatment or instrumented days), with an explicit counterfactual design.
4. Structure / process / outcome indicators inside a functional MEL framework (WHO element 8 output).

Label: **future work**. This panel cannot supply it (`05_hhap_crosswalk.md` element 8; live: “They are not a test of any alert”).
