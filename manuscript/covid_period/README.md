# First hospitalisations after cardiovascular diagnosis in Hong Kong

The [November manuscript](november/Manuscript.pdf) compares first recorded hospitalisations after CHD and HF diagnosis with wider hospital activity from 2013 through 2023. It includes eleven calendar years, three retrospective benchmarks and six weather exposures.
