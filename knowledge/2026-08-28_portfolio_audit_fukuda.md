## 2. Core scholarship artefacts and outputs

### Formal question (from the locked essay)

Estimate monthly associations between six thermal measures and CHD/HF first-hospitalisation counts, 2013–2023; carry heat and cold in parallel; report all twelve comparisons; test whether monthly sums can support a daily-exposure extension.

### What the essay already reports (exploratory; Gate 3 still open)

- All twelve Benjamini–Hochberg *q*-values exceed 0.19.
- Leading exploratory contrasts (Newey–West lag 6): CHD hot nights 1.022 (1.002–1.042) per five extra nights, *q* = 0.192; HF cold days 1.073 (1.006–1.144) per five extra cold days, *q* = 0.192.
- Pre-2020 CHD hot nights 1.011 (0.991–1.032); pre-2020 HF cold days 1.113 (1.053–1.176).
- Daily-recovery calibration (500 replicates) failed frozen F1.2 gates; **no real daily coefficient**.
- Conclusion: hypotheses, not proof.

Do not take these numbers to Fukuda as a confirmatory thermal finding. They are identification results: what monthly aggregates can and cannot support.
