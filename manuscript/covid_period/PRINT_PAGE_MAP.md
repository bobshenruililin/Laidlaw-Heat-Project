## Float contract

- No page break is emitted after a table or a figure. The word processor breaks only when a caption plus its float will not fit.
- Table rows cannot split, and every row but the last keeps with the next row.
- A caption keeps with its float, and the paragraph that names the float keeps with the caption.
- The reference list is the only structural page break (`FLOAT_CONTRACT['page_break_before_references'] = False`).
