# Hong Kong spatial thermal field cache

**Provenance:** `REAL_PUBLIC`. Daily 2 m temperatures from the Open-Meteo ERA5-Land archive, 2013-01-01 to 2023-12-31, `Asia/Hong_Kong`, on a 0.1° lattice plus named landmarks.

These files are **not** HKO station observations. Elevation in the sidecar `.meta.json` is the DEM height Open-Meteo reports at the requested coordinate.

Do not overlay health outcomes on these cells. Do not quote lattice flag totals as HKO yearbook numbers.
