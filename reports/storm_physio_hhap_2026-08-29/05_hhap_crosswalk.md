# WHO 2026 eight core elements × Hong Kong bundle × this panel

Sources: WHO Regional Office for Europe, *Heat–health action plans: guidance*, 2nd ed., 2026; Chong and Law, HKO, December 2023; HKO VHWW/CWW service page; CHP “Beware of Heat Stroke”, 11 August 2025; HAD emergency relief / temporary heat shelters.

| WHO element (2026) | Hong Kong instrument already in force | What this 132-month panel can inform | What it cannot inform |
|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| 1. Governance | Multi-agency bundle (HKO, DH/CHP, Labour, HAD); no single named municipal HHAP evaluated here | That heat and cold already have separate warning authorities | Whether leadership, financing, or coordination is adequate |
| 2. Heat–health warning system | VHWW; Hot Weather Special Advisory; Prolonged Heat Special Alert (a few days of very hot days *or* hot nights while VHWW is in force); Extremely Hot Weather ~35 °C; HKHI | CHD residual sits with official hot-night *counts*, not very-hot-day counts; Prolonged Heat already names nights | A new consecutive-day trigger; whether VHWW “works”; hourly nighttime intensity (Guo’s metric) |
| 3. Populations at increased risk | CHP names infants, elderly, pregnant women, chronic illness including heart disease or high blood pressure, outdoor workers, overweight people | The present cohort (T2D/HTN; first CHD/HF hospitalisation) is already inside that named group | A new named group; age/sex/subtype targeting (strata not delivered) |
| 4. Communication | HKO website/app/broadcast; CHP heat-stroke advice; Labour heat-stress materials | Overnight heat and winter cold both deserve continued public attention; heat-only messaging would omit the stronger HF residual | Message-testing; behaviour change; admissions averted |
| 5. Health-system resilience | Hospital Authority operations (not studied) | Monthly first-event counts are not a surge dashboard | Staffing, cooling of wards, business continuity |
| 6. Reducing heat exposure | Indoor ventilation / air-conditioning advice; HAD temporary heat shelters; longer-term urban form (not studied) | Indoor night temperature is the hypothesis that sleep/autonomic studies point to | Indoor temperature, air-conditioning prevalence, or shelter use in this series |
| 7. Heat–health surveillance | Not a near-real-time first-event CHD/HF feed | Why monthly aggregates cannot guide activation | Event-day surveillance design beyond “need dated, cause-recorded admissions” |
| 8. Monitoring, evaluation and learning | Not performed here | An honest evaluation would need daily cause-recorded data and a predeclared contrast | This paper as an evaluation of warnings or of an HHAP |

Cold-weather counterpart (not a WHO heat element): HKO Cold Weather Warning; HAD temporary cold shelters. The more coherent residual in this file is HF × official cold days (1.073, 1.006–1.144; pre-2020 1.113, 1.053–1.176). WHO heat guidance does not replace that winter instrument.
