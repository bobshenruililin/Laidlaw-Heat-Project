The estimand

One city. One decade. A monthly question.

When a month carries more heat — or more cold — does stroke hospital burden move with it? The answer is not yet on this page. The question is.

  • Design Ecological monthly time series · Jan 2013–Dec 2023 · rate-ratio framing with population offsets.
  • Exposures Mean temperature and extreme-day / spell metrics; air pollution as staged adjustment; population denominators from C&SD.
  • Outcome (inbound) Hospital Authority stroke admission aggregates, governed and cleaned by Roro. No coefficients until QC and merge.
  • Discipline A labelled multi-method panel — not ten press releases. Heat and cold assessed in parallel.
