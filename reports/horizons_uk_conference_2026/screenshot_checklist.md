# Screenshots to attach (22 August 2026)

Capture these **before** sending the email. Save the files. Horizons and, later, Finance will care about the date on the image more than about a sentence in the email.

## Must show in every image

- Search or booking date (browser clock, or a handwritten “22 Aug 2026” in a note app next to the fare if the site hides the date — better: include the URL bar and a system clock).
- Origin **HKG**, destination **LHR** (and the return).
- Dates.
- Cabin: economy.
- **Total including tax**, in HKD if the site will show it. If the site shows GBP or USD, keep the original and write the HKD conversion rate and time in the email body in one line, not as a rounded guess in the subject.
- Airline or agency name.

Do not crop out the total. Do not send a fare that is a “from” banner without taxes.

## Minimum set

1. **Definitional quote (required):** cheapest bookable economy **HKG–LHR–HKG**, out **15 Oct**, back **18 Oct**, return departing **after 14:00**. This is the circular’s date pair. If sold out or every return is before 14:00, screenshot that state.
2. **Fallback only:** cheapest economy out 15 Oct, back **19 Oct**. Attach this so they can *name* a comparator if 18 Oct after 14:00 is unusable. Do not treat it as “lowest” until they name it.
3. **Not the definition:** cheapest economy out 15 Oct, back **23 Oct**. Attach only to show it is no longer the cheap option.
4. Optional: out **14 Oct**, back 18 Oct after 14:00, only if it is cheaper *and* you still keep the 15/18 screenshot as the comparator.

Do not search “any dates covering 16–18 October” and take the minimum. That is not this circular.

## What not to attach

- Business or premium economy.
- Gatwick / Stansted / Luton / City unless you add one extra sentence asking whether those airports are allowed **and** they are clearly cheaper. The circular named Heathrow. Do not buy another airport until they write yes.
- One-way pairs that you have not totalled including tax.
- A screenshot of someone else’s booking.

## After they reply

Keep the same screenshots with the email you sent. If you then buy, the receipt must be in **your** name as payer, and you must keep both boarding passes. Their circular will not pay a claim that cannot show attendance.
