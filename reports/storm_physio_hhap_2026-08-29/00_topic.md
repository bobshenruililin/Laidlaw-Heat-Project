## What the panel identified (not physiology)

Among people with type 2 diabetes or hypertension in Hong Kong, January 2013–December 2023 (132 months), first CHD hospitalisation rose **1.022 (1.002–1.042)** per five additional official hot-night days in a month after mean monthly temperature and other official warning counts (q = 0.192). First HF hospitalisation rose **1.073 (1.006–1.144)** per five additional official cold days (q = 0.192). Very hot days did not leave a CHD residual of the same kind. Pre-2020 HF cold days **1.113 (1.053–1.176)** excluded 1; pre-2020 CHD hot nights **1.011 (0.991–1.032)** included 1.

These are month-count associations after a linear reporting scale `I(count/5)`. They are not consecutive-night duration, not indoor temperature, and not a warning-system evaluation.
