# Pre- vs post-COVID “health improvement” — mechanism memo (quick search)

**Date:** 10 September 2026 (night-cycle citation repair). **Audience:** Bob / later Sol. **Not** a finding from our panel.

Hogan asked for a sense of what evidence and mechanisms other people found that might explain a peculiar pre- vs post-COVID trend. It does not have to be CHD/HF.

## What our file can show

Window-dependent **count ratios** and a **halving of first-event counts**. It cannot show physiological recovery. It has no labs, no person-time, no admission cause.

## Local literature (Hong Kong)

**Utilisation down, deaths up — opposite of physiological improvement.**

## Mechanisms that can look like “improvement” in counts

1. **Hospital avoidance / delayed presentation** (Xin; Wai *Ann Emerg Med*; Wai *JMIR PHS*; Hung survey). Counts fall; community deaths can rise.
2. **First-event depletion** in a 2013–2023 diagnosis window (already live Figure 1). The at-risk set shrinks; this is not incidence.
3. **Influenza and other respiratory co-circulation** collapsed in 2020 in many settings (Cowling et al. 2020 is a neighbour for *R*t, not cardiac recovery). Our flu series is 121/132 months and is a sensitivity, not Model 1.
4. **Pollution decline** 2013–2023 (NO2 53.7→32.1; PM2.5 30.8→14.6 µg m−3) is already in the parked Introduction. Slow, not a 2020-only physiology switch.
5. **Coding and service mix** (Playbook 99 case 4): verify before biology.

## Mechanisms that point the other way

- SARS-CoV-2 cardiac injury and long COVID literature is a **harm** story, not a recovery story (Xie *Nat Med* 2022; Katsoularis *Lancet* 2021; Puntmann *JAMA Cardiol* 2020).
- Deferred emergency cardiac care: Tam et al. 2020 (Hong Kong STEMI delay and worse in-hospital MI composite).

## Primary-source verification and antibody dead end (10 September)

The local utilisation spine was checked against primary full text:

- Xin used weekly 2010–2020 Hospital Authority and death-registry series with
  interrupted negative-binomial time-series models. It found fewer
  hospitalisations and more deaths, particularly out of hospital and among
  people with pre-existing cardiovascular disease. This supports a
  care-seeking interpretation, not recovered physiology.
- Wai *Annals of Emergency Medicine* compared all visits to 18 public
  emergency departments in January–August 2019 and 2020. Visits fell and
  28-day mortality among attenders rose. First author is Wai, not Hung.
- Wai *JMIR Public Health and Surveillance* is a non-COVID emergency-department
  cohort. It likewise pairs fewer visits with more deaths. Its neighbour
  numerals remain outside the manuscript tables.
- Hung is a landline survey of consultation avoidance, not an admissions
  series. The two Tam papers concern delayed STEMI care and a worse in-hospital
  course, not first CHD/HF hospitalisation.
- Cowling et al., *Lancet Public Health* 2020,
  [10.1016/S2468-2667(20)30090-6](https://doi.org/10.1016/S2468-2667(20)30090-6),
  documented a sharp fall in influenza transmissibility during early
  pandemic interventions. It is a co-pathogen shock, not cardiac recovery.

**Killed: antibody production, infection-acquired immunity, vaccination, or
hybrid immunity as an explanation of the 2020 first-event trough.** No direct
evidence was found for this monthly T2D/HTN first-event construction, and the
transfer contains no infection, vaccination, antibody, or serology series.
Hong Kong vaccination studies condition on documented infection and concern
post-infection cardiovascular complications (for example Wan et al.,
*Cell Reports Medicine* 2023,
[10.1016/j.xcrm.2023.101195](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.xcrm.2023.101195)).
That is not improvement in baseline CHD/HF risk. Xie et al., *Nature Medicine*
2022, [10.1038/s41591-022-01689-3](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41591-022-01689-3),
instead found elevated post-acute cardiovascular risk after infection.

## Honest sentence for the COVID-period Discussion

Other people’s Hong Kong files show fewer hospitalisations and more cardiovascular deaths in 2020. That is a care-seeking and system-shock pattern. It is not evidence that cold days repaired hearts in our T2D/HTN first-event panel.
