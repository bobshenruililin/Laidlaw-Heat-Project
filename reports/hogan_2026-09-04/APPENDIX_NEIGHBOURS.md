# Neighbour citations — Bob appendix, not a mechanism tour

One sentence each. These are already in the live Discussion. Do not stack them as support for a residual.

- **Guo et al. 2024.** Daily unplanned emergency hospitalisations, hot seasons 2000–2019. Official hot-night *flag* often null after mean temperature; hourly nighttime excess heat associated. Not first CHD in a diabetes/hypertension cohort, not monthly official *counts*, not 2013–2023 as the sole window.
- **Goggins and Chan 2017.** Daily public-hospital heart-failure admissions and deaths, 2002–2011. Cumulative RR comparing 11 °C with 25 °C over long lags. Not a monthly count ratio per five official cold days, and not a first-event series without admission cause.
- **Chevance et al. 2024.** Ambient heat and sleep, systematic review. Not first CHD hospitalisation in Hong Kong.
- **Ashe et al. 2025.** Extreme heat, sleep, and cardiovascular measures, scoping review. Mixed blood pressure; no demonstration of sleep as a mediator of hospitalisation. Use as a refusal, not as a pathway proof.
- **Liu et al. 2020 and 2026.** Mortality attributable fractions and modelled excess heat deaths. Cannot be rescaled into the present monthly morbidity count ratios.
