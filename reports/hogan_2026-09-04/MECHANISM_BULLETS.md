Mechanisms in the draft. Hypotheses, not estimates from this monthly file.

Keep:
- Interrupted overnight recovery after hot nights. Ambient heat is associated with shorter, more fragmented sleep. A month with more official hot nights can therefore differ from a month that is merely warm on average. This panel cannot see sleep, indoor temperature, or overnight recovery. It only counts nights.
  Chevance et al. 2024 (heat and sleep): https://doi.org/10.1016/j.smrv.2024.101915
  Ashe et al. 2025 (sleep not shown as a mediator of hospitalisation): https://doi.org/10.1186/s13643-024-02742-7
- Cold-related afterload on a failing heart. Cold can raise afterload — the pressure the heart pumps against. A heart already in failure has little reserve. That is a reason the HF residual with official cold days is more coherent than the CHD residual with official hot nights. This panel does not measure afterload, blood pressure, or infection.
  Ikäheimo 2018: https://doi.org/10.1080/23328940.2017.1414014
  Li et al. 2026: https://doi.org/10.3389/fphys.2025.1740919
