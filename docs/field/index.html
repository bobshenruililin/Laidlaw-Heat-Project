A field has modes.

This exploratory page applies eight descriptive methods to the same 90 cells and 4,017 nights; none is treated as confirmatory. The first EOF of de-seasoned Tmin is a common pulse on this grid. The 28°C night is a marine threshold in the same sample. Those are not the same map.

Amplitude vs hot nights

The annual harmonic is larger inland. The 28°C night lives where the mean is high, not where the year swings hardest.

What survives a harder cut

A hot night is not the same object inland

Conditional on a hot night (Tmin ≥ 28°C), how often was the day also very hot (Tmax ≥ 33°C)? On the water, almost never. In the Guangzhou box, almost always. The odds ratio for Guangzhou is large because hot nights are rare there, not because the inland is “more extreme.”
