# About Bob Shen Ruililin

Hi, I'm Bob — a Laidlaw Scholar at The University of Hong Kong, studying **BASc (Global Health and Development)** in the School of Public Health. My summer research sits in Professor David Bishai's lab and asks a deceptively simple question: when Hong Kong's weather turns extreme, how does that show up in **monthly stroke burden**, and how stable is that picture when we refuse to pretend there is only one way to define “heat”?

I am learning that climate–health research is as much about **people and judgment** as it is about models. Weather definitions need Hogan's scientific lead. Stroke aggregates and timing need Roro's governance. Supervision and the multi-method frame come from Dr Bishai. My job is to keep the analysis reproducible, credit collaborators properly, and stay honest about what is ready versus what would be inventing results.
