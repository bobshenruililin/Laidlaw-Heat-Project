## Decision

Retain the completed exploratory count paper as the current evidence-supported manuscript. Do not advertise a physiological improvement, causal COVID effect or changed thermal susceptibility. Its endpoint is first recorded hospitalisation after a relevant diagnosis; admission cause and eligible person-time are absent. The extraction query has not been located or verified. The proposed stronger study asks whether validated disease change can be distinguished from care contact, recording and risk-set change. It proceeds only if verified fields can discriminate explanations and the design adds a specific contribution beyond the closest studies. Nature Cardiovascular Research is an initial scope benchmark, not a promised destination. Adding years, clinical fields or more figures is insufficient novelty.
